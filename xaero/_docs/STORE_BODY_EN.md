Choose rounded-square, hexagonal, octagonal, horizontal, or vertical shapes for Xaero's Minimap.

Press **K** to compare the outlines and select one. The choice takes effect immediately and is saved for the next session. Select **Default** to return to Xaero's own shape setting. The custom outline stays fixed on screen while the map rotates according to its heading setting.

The rectangle options crop the shorter axis of the existing map area. Terrain keeps its original proportions; the cropped axis shows less of the surrounding area. On a widescreen display, the rectangle proportions follow the display aspect ratio.

This add-on requires **[Xaero's Minimap](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/xaeros-minimap)** and **[Minimap Shapes Core](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/minimap-shapes-core)**. The JourneyMap add-on uses the same Core library and stores its selection separately.
